🏥 Healthcare Patient & Financial Analytics Dashboard

An interactive multi-page **Power BI** business intelligence dashboard evaluating patient demographics, medical diagnoses, operational efficiency, and revenue across 55,500+ patient admissions.

## 📊 Dashboard Pages

- **1. Executive Overview:** High-level KPIs tracking total admissions, cumulative billing, average billing per admission, age distribution, and intake trends.
- **2. Patient Demographics & Clinical Analysis:** Deep dive into age cohorts, gender splits, blood type distributions, primary medical conditions, and medication allocations.
- **3. Financial & Revenue Analytics:** Breakdown of the $1.42B billing volume across admission types (Elective, Urgent, Emergency), insurance carriers, and billing distribution tiers.
- **4. Hospital & Operational Insights:** Hospital-level volume metrics, doctor billing distributions, diagnostic test outcomes, and average length of stay.

## 📈 Key Insights & Highlights

- **Admissions Analyzed:** 55,500+ records
- **Total Revenue:** $1.42 Billion with an average of ~$25.54K per admission
- **Demographics:** Average patient age of 51.54 years; senior patients (60+) represent the largest single admission group
- **Admission Distribution:** Urgent (33.61%), Emergency (33.47%), Elective (32.92%)
- **Billing Tiering:** 64.11% of billing generated from the High tier ($908.71M), followed by Medium (31.94%) and Low (3.95%)

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Skills

- **BI Platform:** Microsoft Power BI Desktop
- **Data Modeling:** DAX Measures, dynamic KPI cards, matrix aggregations
- **Data Prep:** Power Query (ETL, type transformation, conditional grouping)
